About GDGOC

Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGOC) are university-based community groups for college and university students interested in Google developer technologies. By joining GDGOC, students bridge the gap between theory and practice.

At COMSATS University Attock Campus, our chapter provides a collaborative environment where students can learn cutting-edge technologies, collaborate on real-world projects, attend expert-led workshops, and grow their professional networks.

Event Highlights

The Kickoff Event brought together enthusiastic student developers, faculty members, and tech leaders for an unforgettable experience. Key moments included:

  • Keynote & Vision Presentation: An inspiring opening talk outlining the mission, roadmap, and upcoming learning tracks for the academic year.
  • Interactive Tech Talks & Demos: Live showcases exploring modern web development, artificial intelligence, and cloud tools.
  • Networking & Team Introductions: Breakout sessions where students met domain leads, asked questions, and signed up for interest groups.

GDGOC Core Teams

Our chapter operates through specialized domain teams dedicated to peer learning and project building:

Web Team

Focuses on frontend and backend web technologies, building responsive web applications, and organizing coding sprints.

App Team

Explores mobile application development using cross-platform frameworks and native tools for Android and iOS ecosystems.

Media Team

Handles photography, videography, graphic design, and visual storytelling for all chapter events and campaigns.

PR Team

Manages public relations, campus outreach, student communications, and external sponsorships.

AI/ML Team

Dives into machine learning models, data science libraries, artificial intelligence research, and hands-on Python projects.

Management Team

Oversees event logistics, scheduling, operations, budgeting, and flawless execution of chapter activities.

Women in Tech Team

Empowers female technologists through dedicated mentorship, networking opportunities, and inclusive tech workshops.
